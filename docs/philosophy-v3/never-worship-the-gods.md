<h1>Never Worship The Gods</h1>

> **NOTE**: This is a composition, take it as such.

---

This is a warning. A reminder. A reckoning of the truth that whenever we called upon high ones to back our claims, whenever we called upon them to arrange a celebration or a slaughter, to gather for a birth of new being or for the death of a witch, to relegate people to submission or elevate them to dominance, to segregate, stratify, stultify, suppress... it was just us. It was always just us.

Perhaps the high ones do exist. Perhaps they watch upon us, meddle with our lives, or... guide us in our journey. Perhaps they see it fit to judge us for our deeds, to punish or reward us. Perhaps they were here, doing great deeds that we remember and revere in myth. Perhaps they are what we say they are, as glorious, or terrible, or both.

Perhaps they are not there.

Do you see their worth and seek to show your admiration, your adoration... through submission? Do you fear them and, in that fear of their power, see their dominance as worth? What are they worth, really? To whom, and for what? And to whomever, for whatever, is that a worth to which you submit, to which your own worth and that of your judgement is subordinate?

Do you believe that? Believe in them?

Then, submit, if you will. But know that in doing so, you are driving blind. To submit to the worth of another is to abdicate your judgement for theirs where it matters most: your judgement about how to hold your life. Whether they are there, or they were never here, never worship them. If they have great power, they may well be terrible for the kind of world they have shaped, if they have little power, they are not gods.

Never worship them, if you value your eyes.
