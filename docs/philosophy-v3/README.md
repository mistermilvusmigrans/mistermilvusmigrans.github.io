# Philosophy V3
## Introduction
Philosophy V3 carries over the metaphysics and epistemology from [Philosophy V1](../philosophy-v1/) (which is also carried forward in [Philosophy V2](../philosophy-v2/)). However, instead of trying to ground values in the same source as truth (i.e. trying to ground ethics and epistemology from one source), I aim to look at what values are in fact (practically) and how they emerge. I posit that values are not grounded in metaphysics the same way epistemology is, but rather, they are grounded in the specifics of our species and self.
