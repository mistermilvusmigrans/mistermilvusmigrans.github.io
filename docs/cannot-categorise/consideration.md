<h1>Consideration</h1>

---

✱

Look up and see the pale sky,

And the merging damp clouds,

Birds soaring so high,

Yet below the sombre shrouds.

...

The day's full of brewing promise

Though a bit drab and cold.

Grey turns blue, and all would miss

The rain the clouds foretold.

✱

They tightened starlight into their eyes,

Saw, frightened, the deep, dark expanse;

Saw they weren't the fulcrum, the focus,

Weren't the cause of the world's advance.

...

But, it was they who looked and learnt,

It was the triumph of sight, not fright.

Yes, they may seem a drop in an ocean,

Yet, the cosmos may well be slight,

...

Slight, if their mind were not alight

With their breath-fuelled flame of cognition.

Dust amid stars but tenfold as bright;

Fragile, fierce, forged in volition.

✱

From hold to hold, crack and crevice,

Straining against the pull of the earth,

Gaining on the peak of the pinnacle,

The moment’s focus our only premise.

...

At long last, we survey the space;

The expanse, above and below.

I'm entranced by the steady rays,

Yes, the sun’s, but also their face,

...

Rays shining from their very being,

Balmed from rigours that wore them thin,

Calmed from the stirring in their heart.

Their glow; oh, what a sight I'm seeing!

...

Etched in their face were daily sorrows,

My heart hurt for their hurtless despair.

I start to forget those sinking days,

As they shine with a thousand tomorrows.
