<h1>Incitation</h1>

---

✱

Never worship the gods.

--- Your eyes must see.

--- Your thoughts must churn.

--- With open eyes and churning thoughts must you act.

Never worship the gods.

✱

Hear this, and the shackles that claim you...

... shall fall away as if they were forged broken.

Hear this, and the sweat that drips from your brow...

... shall water your own garden.

✱

We are all siblings, dancing under the abyss.

I remember this, as I throttle my enemy's throat.

I remember this, as I feel my beloved's flesh.

✱

Rage against fate, it won't care.

Bargain with it, it won't care.

Rage and bargain...

--- For life and lust and greed and trust.

--- For foe and kin and blood, if you must.

✱

Tears for the world are what it deserves.

Let no one tell you the world is a mother.

Let no one tell you to love one another.

...

Watchfulness is what the world deserves.

The dainty flower is seeking to fuck.

The purring cat would slaughter a duck.

✱

With life, the will to kill is entwined,

It is, in the will to life, enshrined.

With life, the will to force is entwined,

It is, in the will to life, enshrined.

...

The child-killer, the mother who fights,

Sharp teeth against solid horns,

The sword-wielder, the shield-bearer,

Sharp points against solid planes.

...

Tormentor's glee and wretch's gore,

Nerve-fire, the burning and burnt.

The lust for blood, the lust in blood,

The greed for dirt and for gold.

...

Nature is patron to you all, you all,

As you offer your blade and your blood,

Your gashes, frenzy, glee and fear,

Your will, schemes, wounds and screams.

...

Your offerings, your will, your life's shape.

---

Burn down the following before proceeding:

- Morality
- Propriety
- Sanctity
- Love and beauty
- Good and evil
- Virtue and vice

Now, let's proceed.
